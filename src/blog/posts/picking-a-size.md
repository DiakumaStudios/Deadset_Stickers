Our sizes are listed as squares, like 7.5 × 7.5 cm. Pick the size that matches the **longest side** of your design. A design that's wider than it is tall will be 7.5 cm wide and a bit shorter.

## A rough guide

- **2.5 to 5 cm:** phone cases, small giveaways, packaging seals.
- **5 to 7.5 cm:** laptops, notebooks and water bottles. The most popular range.
- **10 to 15 cm:** toolboxes, eskys, skateboards and guitar cases.
- **20 to 30 cm:** shopfronts, vehicles and anything you want seen from across the street.

## Watch the small text

If your design has small writing, go up a size. Text under about 2 mm tall gets hard to read once it's printed.
