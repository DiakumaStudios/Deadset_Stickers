Deadset stickers are printed on thick vinyl with a protective laminate, so they're built to handle sun, rain and everyday knocks. Here's how to get the most out of them.

## Before you stick

- **Clean the surface** with a little rubbing alcohol or soapy water, then dry it well.
- Stick at **room temperature**. Very cold surfaces don't grip as well.

## Sticking it on

Peel the backing, line it up, and press from the **centre outwards** to push out any air bubbles. A credit card works great.

## After

Give it **24 hours** before it gets wet. For drink bottles, hand washing keeps them looking newer for longer.
