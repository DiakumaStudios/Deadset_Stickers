# **Meet Deadset Stickers: Custom Stickers Australia-Wide**

Some stickers look great when they arrive, only to start peeling or fading after a short time. Deadset Stickers was created to offer a better option: custom stickers made with durable materials, a straightforward ordering process and attention to the details that matter.

[Deadset Stickers](https://deadsetstickers.com/about/) is a small Australian print shop that started because its team couldn't find stickers tough enough for what they wanted. After finding the ordering process slow and complicated, and stickers that didn't last, they decided to make their own.

The goal is simple: make the stickers they always wanted to buy.

## **Custom Die-Cut Stickers Made to Last**

Deadset Stickers specialises in custom die-cut stickers, cut around the outline of your design rather than left with a standard square background. The result is a sticker shaped to suit your artwork.

The team uses thick vinyl, protective laminate and outdoor signage-grade materials and inks. The stickers are waterproof and UV-protected, helping them stand up to sun, rain and everyday knocks.

Whether you have a logo, illustration or another design in mind, you can customise your order to suit your artwork and preferred sticker size.

## **A Straightforward Ordering Process**

Ordering custom stickers shouldn't be a complicated job. Deadset Stickers keeps the process simple, with clear pricing and an online system that lets you choose your sticker size and quantity, upload your artwork, and select a white or black border.

You can also order multiple designs in one checkout, with individual options for each design.

Once your artwork is submitted, you'll receive a proof showing the cut line to review before printing begins. This gives you an opportunity to check how your design will be cut and approve the proof before your stickers are produced.

Artwork can be supplied in several common formats, including PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG and TIFF. If you're unsure whether your file is ready for printing, the team can help with artwork preparation. Additional artwork work may incur a fee.

## **No Fuss, No Unnecessary Waiting**

Deadset Stickers believes the experience should be as straightforward as the finished product. That means clear prices, a proper proof and a real person to speak to if you need help with your artwork.

Fast turnaround is also part of the way the business works, rather than an extra-cost option. According to its online ordering information, orders are printed and shipped within three business days after the online proof has been approved.

Every order is sent using Express shipping for a flat $13 shipping fee, as stated on the website. Delivery time can depend on the destination.

## **Custom Stickers Delivered Australia-Wide**

You don't need to be local to order from Deadset Stickers. The business services customers Australia-wide, making it possible to turn your artwork into custom die-cut stickers wherever you're based.

For orders over 5,000 stickers, you can contact the team for a custom quote. If you need advice about artwork, have questions about an order or want to discuss a larger quantity, the [team welcomes enquiries](https://deadsetstickers.com/contact/).

## **Your Design, Their Cutter**

Deadset Stickers was built around a simple idea: stickers should look good, be made to last and be easy to order.

With durable materials, a proofing process and a team available to help, the business aims to take the fuss out of custom sticker printing.

Ready to get started? Visit the[ **Deadset Stickers online shop**](https://deadsetstickers.com/shop/) to choose your sticker size and quantity, upload your design and turn your artwork into custom stickers.
