Every die-cut sticker has a thin border where we cut, about 2 mm wide. You can choose white or black, and it makes a bigger difference than you'd think.

## When to go white

- Your design has lots of **dark colours** or a dark outline.
- The sticker is going on a **dark surface**, like a black laptop or bottle.
- You want that classic, clean sticker look.

## When to go black

- Your design is **light or pastel**, and a white edge would blend in.
- Your artwork already has a **bold black outline**, so the border becomes part of the design.
- The sticker is going on a **light surface**.
